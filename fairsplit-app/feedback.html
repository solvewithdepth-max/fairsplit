Our Philosohpy

Even splits are a scam.

You had a salad and water. They had a steak and three mojitos. "Let's just split it evenly" is the biggest lie in dining history. We're here to end it.

Math kills the vibe.

The last thing you want to do after a great meal is open a calculator app and argue about who owes GST. Snap a pic, tap some buttons, and let AI do the nerdy stuff.

Objective > Awkward.

You shouldn't have to choose between your budget and your friendships. By making the split 100% objective, nobody feels cheated. You pay your share, they pay theirs.
